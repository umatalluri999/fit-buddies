🌿 Your 30-Day Wellness Journey

Start with intention, then build healthy habits you can maintain beyond the challenge.

Days 1–3

🥤 Juice-Focused Introduction

Optional Pressed Juicery drinks combined with balanced meals, adequate protein and sufficient calories.

Juice-only cleanses are not medically necessary for detoxification and may not provide adequate nutrition.

Days 4–30

🥗 Sustainable Diet

Build meals around lean protein, vegetables, fiber-rich carbohydrates and healthy fats.

Add appropriate exercise, hydration and sleep.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Fitarya is intended to help people organize wellness activities and encourage one another. We recognize that weight, dietary preferences, health information and activity records can be sensitive.

2. Information We May Collect

When account-based features are enabled, information may include your name, email, team membership, goals, meal logs, activity logs, hydration, weight, and information you choose to provide.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide accounts, team functionality, progress tracking, scores, invitations and related wellness features.

4. Team Sharing

Team participation may involve sharing selected check-in information, activity status, and scores with fellow members. Detailed medical records should not be shared by default.

5. Third-Party Services

Fitarya may use GitHub Pages for hosting and Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. These services have their own policies and terms.

6. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS, authentication and access controls. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users should be able to request deletion of their account and associated personal data, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Account and data deletion controls must be implemented before production registration is opened.

8. Health Information

Medical reports and health information require additional privacy protections. Fitarya will not intentionally publish medical reports to teams.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as Fitarya's features and services change. Material changes should be communicated to affected users.
